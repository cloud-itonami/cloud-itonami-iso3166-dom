# Business model — DOM

Independent public-sector market-entry compliance for the Dominican Republic.

- Dirección General de Contrataciones Públicas (DGCP, órgano rector,
  dependiente del Ministerio de Hacienda) public procurement -- Ley
  47-25 de Contrataciones Públicas (28 July 2025), which replaces Ley
  340-06 (Art. 247 Derogación), and its Reglamento de Aplicación,
  Decreto 52-26 (28 January 2026); SECP (Sistema Electrónico de
  Contrataciones Públicas) e-procurement tooling; comprasdominicana.gob.do
  redirects to www.dgcp.gob.do (see `src/marketentry/facts.cljc`)
- RPE (Registro de Proveedores del Estado) supplier registration --
  does not expire but becomes outdated based on deposited-document
  expiry dates (DGCP's own wording)
- Registro Mercantil (Cámara de Comercio y Producción de la República
  Dominicana) business registration, channeled through the MICM-run
  Formalízate one-window portal
- Dirección General de Impuestos Internos (DGII) tax registration
  (RNC), per el Código Tributario (Ley No. 11-92)
- Ley 47-25 Art. 172 "Reservas de contrataciones" MIPYME
  reserved-contract gate -- bars a bidder who declares a MIPYME-
  reserved-quota award without independently satisfying: a current
  MICM MIPYME certification, women-led claim-consistency (if claimed),
  fiscal/social-security arrears within a 6-month grace window, and
  clearance of a 12-month grave-sanction forfeiture window
